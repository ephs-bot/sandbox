# FiveM / RedM

FiveM (GTAV) and RedM (Red Dead Redemption 2) are community multiplayer
modification frameworks that let players run custom servers with custom game
modes — the technical backbone of the GTA roleplay ecosystem. They power most
of the large GTA roleplay communities, including [[concepts/nopixel]]. For
[[companies/rockstar-games]] they are the primary surface to centralize and
professionalize: the place where most sanctioned-and-illicit creator value
concentrates today.

## State

- **What it is:** custom multiplayer server framework for GTAV (FiveM) and
  RDR2 (RedM); enables custom maps, scripts, economies, and roleplay servers.
- **Role in ecosystem:** the dominant infrastructure layer under GTA roleplay;
  large creator/server-owner economy on top.
- **Strategic relevance:** a 2026 goal is to **centralize + professionalize the
  FiveM ecosystem** within Rockstar's oversight.
