# NoPixel

NoPixel is the most prominent GTA roleplay community/server, built on
[[concepts/fivem-redm]] — the reference point for high-production GTA roleplay
and a major driver of creator/streamer activity. **"NoPixel v"** refers to a
more mainstream GTA roleplay offering that [[companies/rockstar-games]] wants to
validate demand for: taking the NoPixel-style RP experience toward a broader,
more accessible audience under Rockstar's umbrella.

## State

- **What it is:** premier GTA roleplay community on FiveM; sets the bar for
  GTA RP quality and culture; significant streamer/creator gravity.
- **"NoPixel v":** a more mainstream RP offering whose demand Rockstar wants to
  validate (a 2026 goal).
- **Open question:** what "mainstream" means here — accessibility, onboarding,
  audience size, and how it relates to the existing NoPixel community.
