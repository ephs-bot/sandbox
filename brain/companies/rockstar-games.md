# Rockstar Games — Creator Platform

Rockstar Games owns the GTA franchise, home to one of the largest and most
active modding/creator ecosystems in games. Across sanctioned and illicit
destinations, the GTAV creator ecosystem represents **$300M+ of value exchanged**,
historically unofficial and only loosely supported by Rockstar. Ahead of the
**GTA VI PC launch**, the strategic mandate is to mature, centralize, and
professionalize this ecosystem so the GTA VI modding/creator landscape grows
*within* Rockstar's control and oversight rather than outside it. GTA VI is an
unprecedented launch and a new canvas for creators; the goal is to stand up a
GTA VI creator ecosystem on day one using the lessons learned from GTAV.

## State

- **Franchise:** Grand Theft Auto (GTAV today; GTA VI upcoming, PC launch is the
  pivotal moment for the creator/modding ecosystem).
- **Creator ecosystem value:** $300M+ exchanged across sanctioned + illicit
  destinations (GTAV), historically unofficial, loosely supported.
- **Owned creator surfaces:** [[concepts/fivem-redm]] (GTA/RDR multiplayer
  modding frameworks), the broader GTA roleplay ecosystem, and an emerging
  mainstream RP offering tied to [[concepts/nopixel]] ("NoPixel v").
- **Strategic posture:** shift the ecosystem from tolerated/illicit to
  centralized, professionalized, and Rockstar-governed.
- **Owner of this mandate:** [[people/ethan]] (head of creator platform).

## Open threads

- Capture more of the existing **GTAV** creator ecosystem (pull value/activity
  into Rockstar-controlled surfaces).
- Centralize + professionalize the **FiveM** ecosystem.
- Validate demand for a **more mainstream GTA roleplay offering** via
  **NoPixel v**.
- Stand up the **GTA VI creator ecosystem** at/near PC launch, applying GTAV
  learnings.
- Open strategic question: how much to centralize vs. how much to let the
  organic/illicit ecosystem keep its energy — control vs. vitality tradeoff.
